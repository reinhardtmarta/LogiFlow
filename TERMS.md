* Não armazenamos senhas bancárias ou dados confidenciais de cartão de crédito.
* Recomendamos que os usuários nunca compartilhem senhas ou dados bancários pelo chat.

* **Food Safety and Hygiene**: Food sellers warrant that all listed items are wholesome, safe for human consumption, and compliant with applicable food safety standards.
* **LogiFlow Exemption**: The platform and its operators are fully exempt from civil, sanitary, or financial liabilities resulting from food consumption, private transactions, or individual user conduct.

### 3. Optional Software Capacity Packages (> 1,000 Items)
* General platform access and standard food rescue listings are 100% free.
* High-volume industrial producers managing over 1,000 active inventory items may purchase software capacity recharges externally via **Mercado Pago** (with automated webhook confirmation) or **Google Pay**, without auto-renewing subscriptions.

### 4. Privacy and Data Security (GDPR / LGPD)
* LogiFlow follows strict data minimization standards.

* Users must never share passwords or sensitive banking data in the chat.
